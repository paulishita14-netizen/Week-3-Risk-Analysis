# Week 3 – Risk Analysis

## Project Overview

This project performs risk analysis on the Bank Marketing financial dataset.

The objective is to identify potential business, financial, data and modelling risks, assess their likelihood and impact, and propose suitable mitigation strategies.

## Dataset

The project uses the Bank Marketing dataset containing customer-level information related to a bank marketing campaign.

The dataset includes variables such as:

- Age
- Job
- Education
- Account Balance
- Credit Default
- Housing Loan
- Personal Loan
- Campaign Contacts
- Previous Contacts
- Contact Duration
- Subscription Outcome

The target variable is `y`, which indicates whether the customer subscribed to a term deposit.

## Tools Used

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Risk Analysis

The following risks were identified:

1. Data Quality Risk
2. Financial Exposure Risk
3. Campaign Over-Contact Risk
4. Low Subscription / Conversion Risk
5. Class Imbalance Risk
6. Information Leakage / Decision-Timing Risk
7. Limited Historical Data Risk
8. Privacy and Responsible Data Use Risk

## Risk Assessment

Each identified risk was assessed based on:

- Likelihood
- Potential Impact
- Overall Risk Level

## Risk Mitigation

The project proposes mitigation strategies including:

- Data-quality checks
- Customer segmentation
- Campaign contact monitoring
- Appropriate model evaluation metrics
- Careful feature selection
- Additional historical data for long-term forecasting
- Responsible handling of customer information

## Project Files

## Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates how financial and business risks can be systematically identified, assessed and mitigated before using analytical results for decision-making.
